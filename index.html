Current research

  • In preparation
    A Reaction–Diffusion Model for Chronic Wasting Disease Spread in Deer on a Topographically Realistic Landscape, with Optimal Culling Control
    Gabe Flanagin, Alex Escoto, Zhen Chao

    Modeling the spread of chronic wasting disease in deer on real terrain, where slope and land cover shape how deer move. Uses optimal control to find where and when culling effort is most effective.

    Code
  • Ongoing
    Boundary Integral Methods for Interface Problems
    Advised by Zhen Chao

    Boundary integral methods for elliptic interface problems, with applications to electrostatic models and PDEs on complex geometries. Current work focuses on numerical implementation, validation, and analysis of interface formulations.

Publications

  • 2026
    Rationalizing Patterns in the Cation Ordering, Geometric Distortion, and Electronic Properties of a Class of I–V–VI₂ Chalcogenide Semiconductors
    Gabe Flanagin, Robert Berger · The Journal of Physical Chemistry C

    A density functional theory study of the structural and electronic properties of I–V–VI₂ chalcogenide semiconductors, developing a model that relates local bonding motifs and electronic properties to the global geometric structure arising from different cation orderings.

Projects

  • May 2025
    Using Max-Plus Algebra to Compute Reachability in Dynamic Systems
    Undergraduate senior project, advised by Rick Barnard · WWU Scholars Week

    Studied and implemented a max-plus algebra algorithm for approximating solutions to Hamilton–Jacobi–Bellman equations. Proved the method applies to a broader class of functions than previously shown and generalized the semiconvexity results underlying the approach.
